# Online Retail: Customer Analytics & RFM Segmentation
A capstone project segmenting the customers of a UK online retailer (UCI Online Retail dataset):

1. Business understanding
2. Dataset loading and data dictionary
3. Data audit, cleaning and data-quality decisions
4. Recency, Frequency, Monetary (RFM) feature engineering
5. Customer segmentation with clustering, and segment profiles
6. Business recommendations for each segment

**Data:** downloaded with `ucimlrepo` · **Tools:** pandas, scikit-learn, SciPy, seaborn
